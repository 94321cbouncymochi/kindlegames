Endless Dungeon

How to play

Go down floor after floor through a dungeon that never ends. Every floor is new, and the monsters get stronger the deeper you go. When you're defeated, you keep all the gold you found and spend it at camp on upgrades, so your next run can go deeper. The goal is to beat your deepest floor.

The map

  • @ is you (the black square).
  • > stairs down, E elevator.
  • $ gold, ! potion, ? scroll, * treasure chest.
  • ) a weapon and [ armor lying on the floor.
  • & a merchant, + a shrine.
  • Letters are monsters. A capital letter on a black square is a giant boss (every 5 floors). A letter with a double border is a champion, which carries treasure.
  • Dark gray squares are walls. Light gray squares are places you've seen but can't see right now.

Moving and fighting

Use the arrow buttons, or tap a square next to you. Step into a monster to attack it. Every time you act, each awake monster also gets a turn. Nobody can attack diagonally.

Tap a square farther away that you've already seen, and you walk all the way there in one tap. You stop early if a monster comes into view or anything happens.

Wait skips your turn, which is handy for letting a monster come to you.

Monsters

Each kind of monster has its own trick. The list under the map tells you what the ones you can see do.

  • Bat (b) flutters around wildly.
  • Goblin (g) steals gold when it hits. Defeat it to get the gold back.
  • Slime (j) splits into two small slimes.
  • Skeleton (s) shoots arrows from up to 4 squares away in a straight line. Step out of line to stay safe.
  • Wolf (w) moves 2 squares per turn, so you can't outrun it.
  • Ghost (h) floats through walls.
  • Troll (t) heals every turn, so finish it quickly.
  • Mummy (m) poisons you for 3 turns. A potion cures poison.
  • Demon (d) breathes fire from 2 squares away.
  • Some treasure chests are Mimics (c) in disguise!

Levels and perks

Defeating monsters earns experience. Each level gives +4 max HP, +1 attack, and heals you a bit. Every 2 levels you choose one of three random perks, like Vampire (heal when you defeat a monster), Critical (double damage hits), Thorns (monsters that hit you get hurt) or Nimble (dodge hits). The same perk can be picked more than once.

Perks are saved by level. The perk you choose at level 2 is saved for level 2. In every later run, you get it automatically as soon as you reach level 2 again, but not before. Reach a level you've never reached and you choose a new one, which is saved too. At camp, Saved perks lists them all. Tap Change on one to choose again the next time you reach that level.

Weapons and armor

Chests, champions and bosses drop weapons and armor. Step on one to see it next to what you're using, then choose Equip or Leave it. Better gear shows up deeper. Some pieces have a bonus, like a sword "of Leeching" that heals you on every hit, or armor "of Thorns".

Potions and scrolls

  • Potion: heals half your max HP and cures poison.
  • Fire scroll: burns every monster you can see.
  • Freeze scroll: monsters you can see stop moving for 3 turns.
  • Teleport scroll: jumps you to a random spot far away. Great for escaping.
  • Map scroll: shows the whole floor, including the stairs.

Drinking or reading uses your turn.

Special floors

Many floors have a twist, shown next to the floor number: Dark (you only see 2 squares), Treasure (extra gold and a chest), Nest (lots of monsters and gold), Haunted (ghosts), Library (scrolls), Market (a merchant), Shrine, Champion and Boss.

  • A merchant sells a potion, a scroll and a piece of gear for the gold you've found this run. Gold you spend there doesn't go home with you.
  • A shrine lets you either heal fully, or gain a random perk and lose some HP. It works once.

If you walk off a merchant, shrine or piece of gear without choosing, step back on it or tap yourself while standing on it.

The elevator

Every floor has an elevator E right where you arrive. Stand on it and tap Elevator (or tap yourself) to pick any floor from 1 down to your deepest, then tap Go. It doesn't use a turn.

At camp you can also start a run on any floor you've reached. Starting deeper gives you free levels, perks and gear so you aren't a beginner on a hard floor.

Camp and upgrades

Between runs, spend gold on upgrades that last forever: max HP, attack, armor, starting potions, starting scrolls, and Blessing (extra perks at the start of every run, saved just like level perks). Each upgrade costs a bit more than the last.

Saving

The game saves after every move, even in the middle of a run. If your Kindle clears its saved data, you'd lose progress, so open Save code at camp now and then and copy the code somewhere safe. Paste it back in to restore your gold and upgrades.

Tips

  • Fight in corridors so only one monster can reach you at a time.
  • Against skeletons and demons, get close fast or stay out of their line.
  • Save Fire and Teleport scrolls for emergencies, like a boss or a crowd.
  • Upgrades are what get you deeper over time. Even a short run brings home something.
