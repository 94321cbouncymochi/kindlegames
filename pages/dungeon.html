Endless Dungeon

How to play

Go down floor after floor through a dungeon that never ends. Every floor is new, and the monsters get stronger the deeper you go. When you're defeated, you keep all the gold you found and spend it at camp on upgrades, so your next run can go deeper. The goal is to beat your deepest floor.

The map

  • @ is you (the black square).
  • Letters are monsters: r rat, b bat, g goblin, s skeleton, w wolf, o orc, k knight, t troll, m mummy, d demon. A capital letter is a giant boss, found every 5 floors.
  • > is the stairs down, E is the elevator, $ is gold, and ! is a potion.
  • Dark gray squares are walls. Light gray squares are places you've seen but can't see right now, so monsters there are hidden.

Moving and fighting

Use the arrow buttons, or tap a square on the map to take one step toward it. Step into a monster to attack it. Every time you act, each awake monster also gets a turn: it moves one step toward you, or hits you if it's right next to you. Monsters can't attack diagonally, and neither can you.

Tap a square farther away that you've already seen, and you walk all the way there in one tap. You stop early if a monster comes into view or you pick something up, so you never walk into trouble blind.

Wait skips your turn, which is handy for letting a monster come to you.

The elevator

Every floor has an elevator E right where you arrive. Stand on it and tap Elevator (or tap yourself) to pick any floor from 1 down to your deepest, then tap Go. Riding it doesn't use a turn. Use it to skip straight back down to your record, or to go up to an easier floor when you're hurt.

At camp you can also start a run on any floor you've reached. Starting deeper gives you free levels so you aren't a beginner on a hard floor.

Getting stronger during a run

  • Defeating monsters earns experience. Each level up gives +4 max HP, +1 attack, and heals you a bit.
  • A potion heals half your max HP. Drinking one uses your turn.
  • Going down the stairs heals you a little.

Camp and upgrades

Between runs, spend gold on upgrades that last forever: more max HP, more attack, more armor (each point blocks 1 damage from every hit), and extra potions at the start of each run. Each upgrade costs a bit more than the last.

Saving

The game saves after every move, even in the middle of a run. If your Kindle clears its saved data, you'd lose progress, so open Save code at camp now and then and copy the code somewhere safe. Paste it back in to restore your gold and upgrades.

Tips

  • Fight in corridors so only one monster can reach you at a time.
  • Check the monster list under the map. If something hits harder than you, drink a potion early or head for the stairs.
  • Exploring finds more gold and potions, but the stairs are always the safe way out.
  • Upgrades are what get you deeper over time. Even a short run brings home something.
